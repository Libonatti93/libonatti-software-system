01 / THE CHALLENGE

Organizing an operation with many entry points.

Consortium plans, insurance, credit, banking, vehicles and investments create different intents — all of which must reach the advisor with context.

The experience had to turn complex financial products into understandable decisions for individuals and companies without reducing service to a generic sales pitch.

The operational challenge was turning distributed channels into organized opportunities: origin, interest, owner, stage and next step.
