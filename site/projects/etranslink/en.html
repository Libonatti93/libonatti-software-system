01 / THE CHALLENGE

Build the digital home of Brazilian logistics.

Etranslink needed to become more than a blog: an editorial authority, cost radar, and meeting point for the industry.

Shippers, carriers, drivers, industry, agribusiness, and foreign trade operate with fragmented information and urgent decisions.

The product organizes this ecosystem into a trustworthy daily experience designed to generate business connections.
