Nogueira Cardiologia.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Digitize the journey without losing the human care.

The clinic needed more than an online presence. It needed to turn medical authority into a continuous, measurable and simple experience for patients and staff.

The challenge started with acquisition — being found, educating and building trust — and continued into operations: registration, appointments, payments, exams, leads and follow-up.

The solution was designed as a product, not as isolated pages. Every touchpoint feeds the next and gives the team better context to work with.

05 / PROPRIETARY ENGINE

From patient questions to knowledge that builds trust.

Intelligence built around patient intent.

The engine organizes public signals and authorized knowledge in a vector database. RAG retrieves relevant context to support more useful experiences and content—without replacing medical advice, diagnosis or clinical decisions.
